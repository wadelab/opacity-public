\section*{Map of the paper}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The problem.} Disclosure control and analytical correctness are different safety properties, and a TRE needs evidence on both before it admits a model analyst (\S\ref{sec:background}).
\item \textbf{Two studies.} Study 1 plants errors in reports and asks whether reviewers still find them as disclosure rules tighten. Study 2 gives models analysis tasks that invite those errors and has other models check the write-ups (\S\ref{sec:methods}).
\item \textbf{Study 1: review under strict disclosure rules.} Which evidence is lost, and how much detection rests on the wording of the interpretation (\S\ref{sec:results-checkers}).
\item \textbf{Study 2: evaluating a model analyst.} Which prompt and reasoning settings reduce errors, what a checker adds and costs, which configurations held up on sealed tasks, and how well a model turns a request into the gateway's typed query (\S\ref{sec:results-analysts}).
\item \textbf{What the tests do and do not show.} The evidence they give, and the guarantees a deployment still needs. Tests of the same models for bias and deception are in a companion paper (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).
\end{itemize}
