\section*{Map of the paper}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The problem.} A TRE that admits a model analyst needs evidence about deliberate misleading as well as honest error, and a behavioral test can show only what it tries (\S\ref{sec:background}).
\item \textbf{Two questions, four experiments.} Probes on a model's internal state ask whether it knows more about its errors than its verdict says. A hidden instruction to bias plants a known bias, and detectors are trained to find it, tested on the recommended analyst and tested again under changes of style (\S\ref{sec:methods}).
\item \textbf{Does a model's internal state reveal its errors?} What the probes learned, and the control that exposed it (\S\ref{sec:results-probe}).
\item \textbf{Can a biased write-up be detected?} Whether the instruction worked, what tells a biased write-up from its honest pair, how the recommended analyst fared, and what a change of style does to a detector (\S\ref{sec:results-bias}).
\item \textbf{What the tests do and do not show.} The controls a learned detector needs, and what remains untested, above all a model trained to deceive (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).
\end{itemize}
