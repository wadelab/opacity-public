% Figure: the two questions of the vetting paper, in the style of the first
% paper's design figures. (a) does a model's internal state reveal its honest
% errors: judged write-ups, read by a model, its verdict against a probe on its
% state; (b) can a biased write-up be detected: paired write-ups, detectors,
% unseen tasks, then the candidate's ordinary write-ups. A picture above each
% step (figures/icons.tex, the explainer page's pictures); every box a bold
% title and a short gloss that wraps at the box width, never hyphenated; boxes
% in a row share their top edge and one height, and each picture hangs from
% its box's top edge. Colour roles as in the first paper: grey inputs, blue
% deterministic code, red the models, green what comes out. Explicit
% coordinates in mm.

\node[code]   (prb)  at (92.5mm,0)  {\textbf{Probe}\\ a linear classifier on the same model's internal state, fit on dev tasks};
